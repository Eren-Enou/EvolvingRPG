# Curated evolution content workshop

Candidate content, not learned talents or implemented mechanics. This workshop gives future experiments concrete tactical identities without selecting permanent subclasses. Read [the combat lab](COMBAT_EVOLUTION_LAB.md), [mastery design](MASTERY_AND_SPECIALIZATION_DESIGN.md) and [backlog](DEVELOPMENT_EXPERIMENT_BACKLOG.md).

## Selection rules

**Depth-first priority:** use [DEPTH_FIRST_DESIGN.md](DEPTH_FIRST_DESIGN.md) and [HEAVY_DISCIPLINE_DEPTH_STUDY.md](HEAVY_DISCIPLINE_DEPTH_STUDY.md) before promoting these cards. The workshop is an option inventory. Its size is not a target talent count.

| Card | Current research priority | Evidence required for promotion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C01 Retaliatory Momentum | Primary candidate for one deeper lineage | Reliable defensive evidence, useful contextual decision, intentional/readable travel policy |
| C02 Wide Disruption | Dormant | A genuine close-group disruption decision not already served by existing roles/actions |
| C03 Breach and Withdraw | Dormant | Ordinary post-break move/dodge cannot adequately express a worthwhile escape choice; commitment cost remains |
| C04 Measured Step | Dormant | Existing Light movement leaves a demonstrated spacing decision unexpressed; not an input/camera defect |
| C05 Turning Commitment | Dormant alternative, not an automatic C01 upgrade | A bounded preparation-facing decision is useful after fixed-facing travel is reliable |
| C06 Pressing Rhythm | Dormant | Existing one-Light-versus-continued-pressure decisions need a real application, not a compulsory sequence bonus |
| C07 Pursuit Without Haste | Dormant | A distinct retreat-response problem remains after walking, Light and Momentum are understood |
| C08 Set Footing | Dormant | A situational decision cannot be expressed well without a new active tool; no mandatory pre-cast upkeep |
| C09 Charged implement basics | Distant weapon-family experiment | First weapon lineage and complete small loop demonstrate depth and supportable content cost |
| C10 Place-bound practice | Dormant contextual design | An actual environment/combat relationship exists; location alone is not a meaningful technique |

A candidate needs a new decision, credible cost, observable condition and presentation plan. Reject a name that merely restates an existing behavior. Do not make every card a hidden prerequisite for another card. The examples should demonstrate a small authored vocabulary, not hundreds of mandatory connections.

"Discovery context" below proposes why the character might learn of an option. It never awards the option automatically. All effects require deliberate investment when real ownership exists. Any temporary comparison toggle must say it is a trial.

Numbers are intentionally sparse until a specific experiment is assigned. Directional changes, target conditions and once-only consumption must be specified precisely at implementation; an evocative name is not an executable contract.

## C01 — Retaliatory Momentum

**Stage:** detailed candidate, already specified for future V0.4 C.

**Decision:** after a verified evasion, judge whether an advancing Heavy can reach the same opponent before its next threat.

**Benefit/cost:** one extra windup advance on accepted Momentum; additional recovery if that opponent is not hit. The current proposed values are +0.4 m and +0.25 s respectively. No free damage, invulnerability or forced strike.

**Discovery context:** relevant investments plus repeated verified evade/retaliate exchanges. Use [the detailed specification](CONTEXTUAL_DISCOVERY_EXPERIMENT.md), not a separate rule in this card.

**Expression:** a visible return step followed by a committed drive, with a distinct overextended miss pose. Same RMB Heavy action.

**Discard/revise if:** it becomes a compulsory response to every dodge, evidence arrives too late, or the drawback can be bypassed by hitting an unrelated bystander.

## C02 — Wide Disruption

**Stage:** later Breaker alternative.

**Decision:** commit to controlling a close group rather than reaching one distant target.

**Benefit/cost:** a wider Heavy arc with shorter reach and lower per-target HP damage; preserve meaningful planted preparation. This is a different authored profile, not a free arc modifier on every existing Heavy.

**Discovery context:** relevant disruption investment plus real multi-threat encounters where one committed strike applies impact to distinct live opponents. Do not reveal it from duplicating colliders on one enemy or from hitting spawned dummies.

**Expression:** clearly lateral sweep, visible shortened reach, broad impact sound. Cone detection can test the decision before physical blade collision, but the visual must match the actual arc.

**Dependencies:** readable multi-opponent encounters and per-target resolved receipts. Practice remains bounded per action/encounter; more contacts do not multiply all rewards.

**Discard/revise if:** crowd stagger erases all counterplay, the shorter reach is never relevant, or effects outside the visible sweep become common.

## C03 — Breach and Withdraw

**Stage:** exploratory disruption/spacing hybrid.

**Decision:** after creating a guard break, use a limited retreat to leave danger instead of chasing maximum follow-up pressure.

**Benefit/cost:** an optional small backward or lateral displacement during the latter part of that Heavy's recovery, only after its own verified guard break. The actor remains unable to attack or dodge until normal recovery ends. It consumes the hybrid development's commitment and must retain a mechanical drawback, such as longer overall recovery, rather than simply upgrading planted Heavy for free.

**Discovery context:** disruption investment plus repeated guard breaks followed by genuine repositioning away from a second threat. Mere distance after a break is not proof of defensive success; if that evidence is unavailable, use a simpler explicit prerequisite rather than inventing it.

**Expression:** weapon recoil becomes a controlled step out, with no invulnerability cue. The camera stays player-controlled.

**Dependencies:** a committed recovery-movement profile and movement input policy. This is a later extension to the current zero-movement Heavy recovery, not permission to branch directly in the Starter Assets controller.

**Discard/revise if:** it nullifies Breaker's main commitment cost or overlaps completely with simply waiting and dodging. Do not cancel recovery as a hidden convenience.

## C04 — Measured Step

**Stage:** later Light role comparison from the combat lab.

**Decision:** choose spacing with a small directional preparation step rather than always making a large dodge or planted strike.

**Benefit/cost:** limited Light windup movement with reduced poise impact. No invulnerability, no attack cancellation and no automatic target tracking. Direction is committed from actual movement intent at acceptance; Q/E must remain sensible and diagonals normalized.

**Discovery context:** a visible near-future development may be better than a hidden one. Not every useful fundamental needs a discovery riddle. Meaningful Light practice and investment can be enough.

**Expression:** readable footwork connected to a short strike, visibly less forceful than disruption techniques.

**Dependencies:** a directional action-travel context; current forward-only AdvanceDistance is insufficient for arbitrary lateral/backward travel without a designed extension.

**Discard/revise if:** it replaces dodge at no meaningful cost, automatically moves against player intent, or requires constant directional button tricks to remain viable.

## C05 — Turning Commitment

**Stage:** exploratory Heavy handling alternative, not an addition to C01.

**Decision:** decide during preparation whether to spend a limited turn allowance to catch an opponent changing position.

**Benefit/cost:** one bounded facing adjustment during a clearly defined early windup interval, at the cost of narrower contact arc or longer preparation. Once committed to active impact, facing locks. Camera movement alone must not silently steer it.

**Discovery context:** could follow invested advancing technique and encounters with lateral movement. First test as a visible debug alternative; discovery evidence is not worth implementing until the behavior is independently useful.

**Expression:** anticipation visibly redirects before the final committed swing. Do not snap the weapon at contact.

**Dependencies:** explicit facing intent and phase limits. Current snapshots lock the relevant direction; a designed turn profile is new work.

**Discard/revise if:** it becomes universal soft lock-on, makes positioning irrelevant or causes the player to fight the camera. Keep the ordinary fixed-facing option viable.

## C06 — Pressing Rhythm

**Stage:** later Light-pressure identity.

**Decision:** continue deliberate pressure against the same opponent, or disengage before the next threat.

**Benefit/cost:** a short-lived optional advancing Light opportunity after separately completed relevant contacts, with exposure on a miss. No forced third action, passive escalating damage stack or free cancellation.

**Discovery context:** sustained meaningful Light exchanges with an invested pressure development. Do not count holding LMB, attempted attacks while busy, or multiple collider callbacks as a sequence.

**Expression:** progressively purposeful footwork, with a clear overreach/recovery when the player chooses the advancing opportunity and misses.

**Dependencies:** same-opponent source sequence, finite opportunity, accepted-action consumption and neutral behavior when the player stops. Use the lab's small trial before authoring a deep branch.

**Discard/revise if:** it creates the mandatory three-hit rotation the combat foundation was meant to avoid. Optional efficiency can still become mandatory in practice, so ask what reason the player has to stop.

## C07 — Pursuit Without Haste

**Stage:** exploratory answer to a retreating opponent.

**Decision:** follow a visible retreat without reflexively using a long advancing Heavy.

**Benefit/cost:** a selected Light application extends only its windup travel toward a retreating engaged opponent, retaining its normal reach and adding a meaningful miss exposure. It does not teleport or guarantee contact. It competes with another Light development rather than stacking by default.

**Discovery context:** actual observed opponent retreats after a live exchange, plus a relevant Light investment. Increasing separation alone is insufficient: the player may be walking away. Prefer an authored retreat state from the enemy if this rule is used.

**Expression:** a controlled pursuit step and compact strike, distinct from Momentum's larger drive.

**Dependencies:** credible retreat attribution and collision-aware target-bound travel intent. It must not force auto-navigation around walls.

**Discard/revise if:** its condition is too narrow to be worth a development, or it merely duplicates Momentum with a different button.

## C08 — Set Footing

**Stage:** future active-ability comparison; also described in [equipment and abilities](EQUIPMENT_ABILITIES_AND_COMBAT_EXPRESSION.md).

**Decision:** spend an opening preparing a more stable Heavy, or attack/reposition now.

**Benefit/cost:** exposed setup grants one brief reduction in incoming poise impact during the next accepted Heavy windup. HP damage and later recovery remain consequential. No protection after expiry, no refund for failed commitment, and no automatic attack.

**Discovery context:** ordinary disruption investment can expose it as a visible option. Do not require being hit repeatedly for all defensive growth; such a rule would encourage intentional damage taking.

**Expression:** a planted setup and a visibly sustained brace, with clear expiry. It must not resemble an invulnerable shield.

**Dependencies:** ability action phases, opportunity acceptance and poise-effect composition. A new input must not replace existing technique debug controls silently.

**Discard/revise if:** players always activate it before every Heavy or never have a realistic setup opening. Compare a no-new-button alternative first.

## C09 — Charged implement basics

**Stage:** much later weapon-family prototype, not the next Warrior task.

**Decision:** release a prepared basic shot early for responsiveness or hold for a more committed result.

**Benefit/cost:** continuous hold/release changes one authored action profile within bounded ranges. A separate Heavy remains a committed cast. Do not immediately add an elemental reaction system, mana economy and extensive spell catalog.

**Discovery context:** initially a visible weapon-family foundation. Later interactions may depend on meaningful releases/casts, but first prove that aim, charging, interruption and movement feel coherent.

**Expression:** clear charge stages and release, visible projectile path, readable cast recovery. No arbitrary damage before the shot arrives unless it is explicitly a hitscan action.

**Dependencies:** aim origin, projectile/obstruction contract, charge cancellation, input focus loss, cost timing and release behavior. The current click-accepted melee action is not automatically a charged-input implementation.

**Discard/revise if:** holding hides commitment or charge power becomes strictly best at all ranges. Test early release and interrupted charge without account or talent progression first.

## C10 — Place-bound practice

**Stage:** contextual discovery design, no selected combat effect.

**Decision:** investigate a repeatable location/encounter clue rather than repeat the same attack everywhere.

**Benefit/cost:** a regional experience contributes knowledge about an application, but ownership still requires relevant character development. The region does not grant a permanent universal damage bonus merely for visiting.

**Discovery context:** an authored environmental condition that actually affects the fight, such as repeated use of cover against a clearly visible threat. A location tag alone is a quest flag, not evidence of a learned combat interaction.

**Expression:** a journal observation connecting place, experience and technique. Revisit options remain available; missing the first run cannot permanently invalidate a character fantasy.

**Dependencies:** a real environmental mechanic and a repeatable encounter source. Defer until such content exists; do not invent inferred terrain skill from coordinate checks.

**Discard/revise if:** it becomes an obscure mandatory sightseeing checklist or forces a whole build to wait for a rare world state.

## Ideas deliberately not promoted

| Idea | Why it is weak now | What evidence could reopen it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| More poise immediately after full stagger | May have no useful target consequence | Different poise-recovery or multi-target context; see timing worksheet |
| Automatically attack after successful dodge | Removes player choice and may commit into danger | No current reason to reopen; a distinct intentionally automated ability would need explicit design |
| Every guard break emits a new damaging pulse | Expands damage/proc/credit recursion before a need is proven | A specific crowd-control decision and bounded non-recursive contract |
| Infinite recovery-reduction ranks | Eventually erase exposure or become imperceptible | A bounded refinement is already the safer experiment; infinite mechanical value remains unsolved |
| Randomly generated talent from any two tags | Combinations can be incoherent and unreadable | A small validated authored grammar and content cost evidence |
| Build-based enemy immunity | Punishes success and invalidates identity | Use explicit counterplay/alternative openings instead |
| Gear grants a complete deep branch | Bypasses character investment and replay identity | A constrained temporary preview item is different and must be labeled |

## One-card implementation discipline

Select a candidate because a current test exposed a missing decision. Write its exact trigger, eligibility, snapshot fields/context, outcome policy, drawback and reset behavior. Add one comparison scene and only the supporting code that candidate uses. Plan its animation coverage before treating it as complete.

After a trial, archive the result even if the effect is discarded. Validated evidence or presentation infrastructure can survive a rejected content idea; a rejected idea does not justify keeping unused gameplay branches.

None of these cards is implemented, purchased, saved or tested by this document.
