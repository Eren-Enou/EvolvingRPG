## Test it in Unity

1. Open `Assets/Game/Scenes/RelayOutpost.unity` in the existing Editor. Press Play and click Game view. No scene generation or Input Actions configuration is required.
2. Walk to the gold board and press **F**. Select **Restore Relay**, fight both defenders, go around the center barrier and press **F** beside the blue relay. Try **Q/E**, **Space**, mouse look/zoom, **LMB/RMB** and **Left Shift** during the route.
3. Inspect development, preview and confirm one specialization, and explicitly save in an unused slot. Stop and restart Play, load that slot and repeat the expedition. Confirm your technique survives reload and there is no second completion point. Use only a slot you intend to write.
4. Try Priority Interdiction: move wide right before going north, or approach through the middle to draw the guard. Check that the debrief reflects your route. Try defeat or abandon with **F** at the arrival beacon and retry.
5. Use another empty slot for an independent Warrior. Earn the other specialization, compare the same guard and fast encounters, and switch between the saves. In **Saved Warriors**, check the listed builds, review and cancel a load, then review and confirm it. With Breaker, note when you choose Light or movement over a longer commitment.
6. To compare Momentum returns, replay Restore Relay with Momentum and approach one fighter at a time. Dodge after the enemy commits, then use Heavy against that fighter within 1.5 seconds of recovering from the dodge and finish the Heavy recovery. The board's optional combat diagnostics show progress and rejection reasons before the reveal. After three qualifying exchanges, return to the hub, learn the revealed option, equip it separately, and save. In Priority, try one armed opening with no forward input and another with forward + RMB. The opening identifies the linked fighter; check whether you can tell when the extra travel is useful and when you would prefer to stop short.

Automated: **Tools > EvolvingRPG > Run Extended Regression Checks** from one saved, stopped scene. **Run Live Relay Route Check** isolates the walking/fighting/reload route. `tools/RunUnityChecks.ps1 -Stages '13,14'` launches one dedicated interactive Editor when none is open. Do not use it while editing; the simulation supplies input without desktop control. Reports: `Logs/V06-SavedWarriorsRegression.txt`, `Logs/ExtendedChecks.txt`, `Logs/ExpeditionChecks.txt`, `Logs/LiveRelayRouteChecks.txt`.
