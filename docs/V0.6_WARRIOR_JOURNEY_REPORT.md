## Changed areas

- `ExpeditionLoop.cs` and `SandboxEnemy.cs`: station and combat presentation, return report, contract anchors and discipline-specific practice suggestions. `ExpeditionLoop.cs` also resets camera collision damping on zone changes.
- `OwnedCharacter.cs` and `DevelopmentPanel.cs`: immediate notification after validated record replacement, plus explicit save/load status based on the in-memory revision.
- `CharacterSaveStore.cs` and `CharacterSaveChecks.cs`: protect inaccessible files and verify that a failed replacement preserves both copies.
- `PlaygroundMouseLook.cs` and `ExpeditionChecks.cs`: restore the current Move value when the player clicks to capture the cursor and verify the transition through native virtual devices.
- `LiveRelayRouteChecks.cs`, `ExpeditionChecks.cs`, `ExtendedChecks.cs`, `AutomationRunner.cs` and `tools/RunUnityChecks.ps1`: explicit focused or full runs, real route comparison and recovery checks. Player build smoke is in `RelayPlayerSmoke.cs`; batch build entry is in `ExpeditionBuild.cs`.
- No new scene, third-party change, stamina adjustment, equipment economy, or extra owned ability. `RelayOutpost.unity` already contains the needed references.

## Remaining work

Physical keyboard and mouse acceptance, first-time readability, route preference, natural discovery and combat feel are for player testing. The next strongest development task is to compare when players intentionally take or decline the existing return, and where the evidence rules reject an exchange they expected to count. Use that evidence to decide whether the game needs clearer cues, a more legible encounter, or one distinct handling refinement. No economy expansion or new evolution is included in this checkpoint.
